About us

Three decades of designing with purpose.

S.Kumar & Associates is a multi-disciplinary practice of architects, engineers, interior and landscape designers and project managers based in New Delhi.

We deliver designs that bring together science, art and innovative sustainability techniques — guiding each project through planning, design, construction and operation of buildings.

From homes and institutional campuses to interiors and landscapes, one team carries a project from the first sketch to the finished building, so the design intent survives all the way to the site.
