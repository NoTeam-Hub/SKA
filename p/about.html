About us

Nearly four decades of designing with purpose.

Founded in New Delhi over 38 years ago, S. Kumar & Associates is an integrated practice of architects, engineers, interior and landscape designers, and project managers.

We specialize in translating complex briefs into enduring public infrastructure, institutional campuses, and bespoke living spaces. By keeping every discipline under one roof, we eliminate the friction between concept and construction—ensuring seamless execution from master planning to final handover.
