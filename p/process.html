Our work process

The steps of successful project completion.

Six phases, one team. Here is what we do — and what we ask of you — at every stage, from the first brief to the final handover.

01Conceptual Design02Schematic Design03Design Development04Construction Documents05Construction Bidding06Construction Administration
Architect & interior designerClient
01

Milestone

Conceptual Design

We explore your vision, site constraints, and budget to establish a clear creative direction and functional program for your project.

Brief & concept

Architect & interior designer

  • Client brief & programme of requirements (architecture + interiors)
  • Site visit, site evaluation & documentation
  • Review of building bye-laws, zoning, FAR & setbacks
  • Budget analysis & rough cost estimate
  • Topographic survey & soil investigation coordination
  • As-built drawings of existing structures
  • Concept sketches & interiors mood boards

Client

  • Complete the client questionnaire
  • Share budget, aspirations & reference imagery
  • Provide site documents (title, survey, approvals)
  • Sign the letter of appointment
  • Approve programme & concept

Checkpoint — Approval of brief & concept

02

Milestone

Schematic Design

We translate ideas into preliminary floor plans, 3D massing, and early cost estimates so you can visualize the layout and select a contractor.

Preliminary drawings

Architect & interior designer

  • Site plan & floor plans (architecture + interiors)
  • Sections & principal elevations
  • 3D massing, models & interiors concept images
  • Preliminary cost estimate
  • Advice on appointing structural, services & other consultants
  • Contractor recommendation

Client

  • Review & approve floor plans
  • Budget review
  • Appoint consultants
  • Contractor shortlisting & selection

Checkpoint — Approval of schematic design

03

Milestone

Design Development

We refine the architecture and interiors by locking in exterior 3D models, room layouts, finishes, fixtures, and updated pricing.

Outline specifications

Architect & interior designer

  • 3D model of exterior & full-dimension plans, sections & elevations
  • Coordination with structural, electrical, plumbing, HVAC & fire consultants
  • Reflected ceiling plans
  • Door & window locations and schedules
  • Material specifications & interior finish palette (source materials)
  • Refined interior layouts, fixtures & furniture plan
  • Cost estimate revision with contractor / quantity surveyor

Client

  • Review the updated cost estimate
  • Approve materials & finishes palette
  • Approve the design at a formal presentation

Checkpoint — Approval of 3D design

04

Milestone

Construction Documents

We produce detailed technical drawings and written specifications that contractors and engineers use to build the project accurately.

Technical drawings & specs

Architect & interior designer

  • Working drawings: plans, sections, elevations & large-scale details
  • Consultant drawings merged & coordinated (structure, MEP, HVAC, fire)
  • Written specifications & schedule of quantities
  • Cabinets, joinery & built-ins design
  • Hardware, lighting, fixture & accessory selection
  • Tender drawing set

Client

  • Approve finishes, fixtures & appliances
  • Confirm the drawing set for tender
05

Milestone

Construction Bidding

We assist with permit submissions, answer contractor questions, and review final bids so you can sign your construction contract with confidence.

Building permit & tender

Architect & interior designer

  • Tender documents & contract form; contractor shortlisting
  • Drawings & support for statutory approvals / building permit
  • Pre-bid meeting & answers to contractor queries
  • Tender analysis, comparative statement & recommendation
  • Negotiation support & contract documents

Client

  • Sign the application for statutory approvals
  • Approve hardware, lighting & cabinet selections
  • Select the contractor
  • Sign the construction contract & issue work order
06

Milestone

Construction Administration

We monitor construction on-site, review contractor shop drawings, and conduct final walkthroughs to ensure the built work matches the design.

Site observation

Architect & interior designer

  • Issue execution drawings, details & clarifications
  • Periodic site visits & quality checks
  • Review of shop drawings, samples & mock-ups; work with fabricators
  • Review of contractor schedule; advice on time & cost changes
  • Certification of contractor bills
  • Punch list & final walkthrough
  • As-built drawings & completion / occupancy certificate support

Client

  • Attend site meetings with contractor & architect
  • Approve samples, variations & changes
  • Release payments to contractor
  • Final inspection & handover

Checkpoint — Final review & handover
