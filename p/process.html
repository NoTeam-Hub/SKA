Our work process

The steps of successful project completion.

Six phases, one team. Here is what we do — and what we ask of you — at every stage, from the first questionnaire to the final review.

01

Milestone

Conceptual Design

We explore your vision, site constraints, and budget to establish a clear creative direction and functional program for your project.

Research

Architect & interior designer

  • Program (architecture + interiors)
  • Budget analysis
  • Zoning review
  • As-built drawings
  • Interiors concept development

Client

  • Client questionnaire
  • Provide imagery
  • Approve program
02

Milestone

Schematic Design

We translate ideas into preliminary floor plans, 3D massing, and early cost estimates so you can visualize the layout and select a contractor.

Preliminary site plan

Architect & interior designer

  • Floor plans (architecture + interiors)
  • Contractor recommendation
  • Preliminary cost estimate
  • 3D massing
  • Interiors conceptual images

Client

  • Floor plan approval
  • Contractor selection
  • Budget review
03

Milestone

Design Development

We refine the architecture and interiors by locking in exterior 3D models, room layouts, finishes, fixtures, and updated pricing.

Outline specs

Architect & interior designer

  • 3D model of exterior
  • Reflected ceiling plans
  • Consultant’s drawings
  • Cost estimate revision by contractor
  • Develop interior finish palette (source materials)
  • Refine interior layouts
  • Finishes, fixture & appliance selection

Client

  • Cost estimate review

Checkpoint — Approval of 3D design

04

Milestone

Construction Documents

We produce detailed technical drawings and written specifications that contractors and engineers use to build the project accurately.

Technical drawings & specs

Architect & interior designer

  • Hardware, lighting & accessory selection
  • Cabinets & built-ins design
  • Detail drawings

Client

  • Finishes, fixture & appliance approval
05

Milestone

Construction Bidding

We assist with permit submissions, answer contractor questions, and review final bids so you can sign your construction contract with confidence.

Building permit

Architect & interior designer

  • Contractor to provide final pricing
  • Review shop drawings
  • Work with fabricators
  • Answer contractor questions

Client

  • Hardware, lighting & accessory approval
  • Cabinets & built-ins approval
  • Contractor contract
06

Milestone

Construction Administration

We monitor construction on-site, review contractor shop drawings, and conduct final walkthroughs to ensure the built work matches the design.

Observation

Architect & interior designer

  • Provide additional drawings & design
  • Review shop drawings
  • Punch list

Client

  • Site meetings with contractor & architect

Checkpoint — Final review
